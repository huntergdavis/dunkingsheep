usage: dunkingsheep pr wait [-h] [--head HEAD] [--author-task AUTHOR_TASK]
                            [--list] [--cancel CANCEL] [--json]
                            [target]

positional arguments:
  target                owner/repo#PR

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --head HEAD           full expected head SHA
  --author-task AUTHOR_TASK
                        declare authorship using your identity-bound live
                        work-task lease; requires --head
  --list                list your waits and poll timestamps
  --cancel CANCEL       cancel your wait by ID
  --json
